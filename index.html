Research Interests

My research interests now lie at the intersection of Security and large language models. Generally, I'm strongly interested in:

  • Agent Security | Creating secure Agent frameworks or make personal assistants like openclaw more secure
  • Red vs. Blue Team Exercise | Exploring more effective attack and defense strategies for llm
  • Safety Training | Innately build helpful, harmless and honest model
  • Alignment for human values and intentions | Ensuring that AI systems align with human values and intentions
  • Model Mechanistic Interpretability | Opening the black box: understanding how knowledge, reasoning, and behavior emerge from the internal circuits and representations of large language models.
